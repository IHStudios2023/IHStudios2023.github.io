QWERTY DJ – Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 23, 2026

QWERTY DJ ("the app") is developed by IH Studios. This policy explains what information the app collects and how it is used.

Information the app collects

  • DJ name and scores. If you choose to submit a score to the global leaderboards, the app sends your chosen DJ name, your score, accuracy, star rating, the song and difficulty, and a random device identifier generated by the app. This identifier is not tied to your Apple ID, phone number, email, or any other personal information. Leaderboard data is stored using Supabase.
  • Game progress. High scores, unlocked songs and cosmetics, and settings are stored only on your device.

Advertising

The app shows ads served by Google AdMob. Google may collect device information, advertising identifiers, and usage data to serve and measure ads, as described in Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. On iOS, the app asks for permission through App Tracking Transparency before using your advertising identifier; if you decline, you will still see ads, but they will not be personalized.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Data deletion

You can clear all local progress from the app's Settings screen. To have your leaderboard entries removed, email the address below with your DJ name.
